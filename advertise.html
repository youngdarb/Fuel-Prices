Who sees your ad

GSY Fuel Prices is where islanders check what fuel costs before they fill up. Most visitors come back regularly, usually around the days prices change, and almost all of them drive.

If your customers are Guernsey drivers or households, this is a small, local and fairly captive audience. It works well for garages, tyre and repair shops, valeting, driving instructors, taxi firms, trades and anyone else selling to island residents.
